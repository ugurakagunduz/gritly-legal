Gritly Pro subscription / aboneliği

  • Manage or cancel: open the app → Settings → Gritly Pro → Manage or cancel subscription, or use App Store → Subscriptions.
  • Restore purchases: app → Settings → Gritly Pro → Restore purchases (or the paywall).
  • Aboneliği yönet/iptal: uygulama → Ayarlar → Gritly Pro → Aboneliği yönet / iptal et veya App Store → Abonelikler.

Privacy and data / Gizlilik ve veriler

  • No account is required. Quotes, saves, likes, streak and preferences stay on your device.
  • Quote reports and community like counts are sent anonymously only when you start them.
  • Privacy policy / Gizlilik politikası: privacy-policy.html
  • Data deletion request / veri silme talebi: email us at the address above.

Widgets not updating? / Widget güncellenmiyor mu?

  • Remove and re-add the widget after installing a new version.
  • Open the app once so the widget can refresh its timeline.
  • Widget'ı kaldırıp yeniden ekleyin ve uygulamayı bir kez açın.
